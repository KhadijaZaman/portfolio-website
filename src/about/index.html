The specialist behind the results

I make brands visible where people search now — on Google, and inside the AI engines that increasingly answer for them: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews. SEO, content, and automation, run as one compounding system rather than four separate hires.

I'm Khadija Zaman, Marketing Manager at Wellows. I own SEO, content strategy, AI-powered automation, and influencer partnerships end to end — with one goal: making brands visible wherever people search, from Google results to the answers ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity now hand back directly. My work grew GSC impressions from 680K to 3.78M and increased LLM referral traffic 22× in 12 months.

I also build the tools that fill gaps in the space: three public instruments grounded in Google patent research, used by 1,647 practitioners across UK, US, and EU markets, acquired entirely through organic search.

Beyond in-house work, I write as a bylined contributor at AllAboutAI.com, publishing SEO and AI-search articles that reach a large, AI-focused audience — a big share of the search impressions above comes from that authored work.

My core specialism is semantic SEO and the emerging discipline of AI search — making brands not just rank in Google, but get cited inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. I pair that with hands-on automation (Python, n8n, LLM orchestration) so strategy actually ships, repeatably, without a bloated team.

I hold a Train the Trainer certification from FranklinCovey, which shapes how I brief teams, document systems, and bring technical strategy into executive conversations.
