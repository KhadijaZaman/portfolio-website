Khadija Zaman, SEO and AI search lead

Khadija Zaman is an SEO and AI search (GEO/AEO) lead based in Karachi, Pakistan. She has worked in search since 2019, in agency and in-house roles, and since 2023 has owned the SEO, content and AI-search programs at Wellows, a B2B SaaS company, where her formal title is Marketing Manager. She builds SEO tooling grounded in Google patent research, used by 1,647 practitioners.

In-house at Wellows I lead SEO, content strategy, AI-search visibility and the automation underneath them, end to end; the title on my contract is Marketing Manager, the work is technical. The goal is one thing: making brands visible wherever people search, from Google results to the answers ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity now hand back directly. Over the last 12 months that grew Search Console impressions from 680K to 3.78M, and over eight of them took LLM referral sessions from a 176-a-month baseline to roughly 9× (GA4); the case study shows the working. For a separate, confidential client, LLM referral traffic grew 22× over 12 months (GA4).

I also build the tools that fill gaps in the space: three public instruments at Wellows grounded in Google patent research, used by 1,647 practitioners across UK, US, and EU markets, acquired entirely through organic search. The engines and surrounding tooling are open source on GitHub; the full list is on the tools page.

Beyond in-house work, I write as a bylined contributor at AllAboutAI.com, publishing SEO and AI-search articles that reach a large, AI-focused audience — a big share of the search impressions above comes from that authored work.

My core specialism is semantic SEO and the emerging discipline of AI search — making brands not just rank in Google, but get cited inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. I pair that with hands-on automation (Python, BigQuery, n8n, LLM orchestration) so strategy actually ships, repeatably, without a bloated team. I also test my own claims: in 2026 I rebuilt my published cross-engine citation study, ran it against a permutation null, and retracted two headline findings that did not survive.

I hold a Train the Trainer certification from FranklinCovey, which shapes how I brief teams, document systems, and bring technical strategy into executive conversations.
