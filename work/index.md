### AI visibility from near-zero: 9× LLM traffic in 8 months on $0 paid spend

Wellows had Google traffic but near-zero presence inside AI answers and no citation footprint. From a 176-sessions-a-month baseline, LLM referral sessions reached roughly 9× over the eight months the case study covers.
